Huihui Li (李晖晖) | Assoc. Prof.

Dr. Huihui Li is an Assoc. Prof. at Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an, China. She received the M.S. and Ph.D. degrees from Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an, China, in 2003 and 2007, respectively. Her main research interests are computer vision, pattern recognition, and remote sensing image image processing and analysis. She is or has been a reviewer for IEEE TVCST, ACM MM, Journal of Automation, Control and Decision Making, etc. She is a expert in correspondence evaluation for the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and is a member of China Graphics Society, China Artificial Intelligence Society, Automation Society.

Research

My current work mainly involves computer vision, pattern recognition, and deep learning. Specifically, it includes the following aspects:

  • Multimodal data learning and fusion:
    • Cross-modal graphic matching and retrieval;
    • SAR and multispectral image fusion and conversion generation;
    • Target detection and recognition based on visual relations.
  • UAV intelligent visual perception (visual navigation, localization and tracking).
  • Vision-based intelligent monitoring and state detection of unattended industrial scenes.
